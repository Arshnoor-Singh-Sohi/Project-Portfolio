These notes follow the order of study.

## How to use these notes

1. Open an area folder in the tree, for example **Maths**.
2. Open a subject folder inside it.
3. Read `00 Progress` first. It shows the last session, the mistakes and the weak areas.
4. Open **Review** to see the notes that are due for review today.
5. In a note, answer each check question before you reveal the answer.

## Papers

The Papers area explains research papers from first principles. It has one folder for each topic, and one folder for each paper inside its topic. Open a paper folder first. It has the citation, the result in three sentences and the reading order. Then read "00 The paper on one page". [How to read a paper](/notes/papers/reading-method) explains the method and the pages of every paper folder.
