## How a figure or animation is made

1. Each one fixes one named misunderstanding, or shows one structure that text explains badly.
2. Each figure is an SVG file. It was checked for overlaps, cut-off text and wrong numbers before it was saved.
3. Each animation was checked frame by frame before and after rendering.

Status values: **planned**, **rendered**, **in lesson**.
