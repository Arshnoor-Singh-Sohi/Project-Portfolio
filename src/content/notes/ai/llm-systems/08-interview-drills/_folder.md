This part rehearses the deep-dive format. Each drill starts with a broad question and goes five levels deep, with model answers hidden in check blocks. L5 holds every formula and number on one page, and L6 is a full mock interview. Do each drill after the parts it covers: the [course plan](/notes/ai/llm-systems/course-plan) gives the day.
